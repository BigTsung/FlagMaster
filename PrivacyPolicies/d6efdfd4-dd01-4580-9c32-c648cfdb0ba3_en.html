FlagMaster Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the native iOS and iPadOS version of FlagMaster.

Data stored on your device

FlagMaster saves your answer counts, review progress, and preferences on your device to run the game and resume your progress. When you upgrade from the previous version, the app can read your existing answer history and preferences from the same device.

Data collection

FlagMaster does not transmit this data to the developer. The app does not require an account, include advertising or analytics services, or track you across apps or websites.

Deleting your history

Open Settings, select Save, and choose Clear all answer history to reset your passport and review progress. You can remove the app and its local data by deleting FlagMaster from your device.
